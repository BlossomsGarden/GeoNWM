In Figure A we show short-horizon Cityscapes [15] predic-
tions from DINO-world [3], Cosmos-12B [1], and Delta-
World. All three models predict the car moving out of the
frame, but both DINO-world and Cosmos fail to maintain
the bicycle wheel, DINO-world also loses the sign post, and
Cosmos misses some of the people in the background.

   In Figure B we show mid-horizon KITTI [24] predic-
tions, comparing mean and best samples for Cosmos-12B
and DeltaWorld. Both models track the car's motion, but
DeltaWorld 's best sample is more accurate than Cosmos's:
for example, it provides a more accurate depth estimate on
the passing train. Cosmos also yields mean and best samples
that are very similar, reflecting lower variation across its
outputs.

back into pixels for the RGB visualization.
